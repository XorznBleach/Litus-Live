TRANSPARENCY

Data sources & limitations

What this prototype does—and does not—claim.

Satellite imagery

The proposal identifies Copernicus Sentinel-2 as a potential data source. This front-end prototype does not connect to Sentinel-2 or perform image classification; its map markers are sample records.

Oceanographic conditions

Wind and current data are not live-integrated. A future version would need to verify dataset coverage, update frequency, terms, and suitability before estimating drift or accumulation risk.

Ground verification

Cloud cover, sun glint, coastal water conditions, and resolution can affect observations. All potential detections should be treated as indicators for inspection, not confirmed shoreline events.

Prototype privacy

Reports created here are stored locally in your browser. There is no server, authentication, shared database, or alert delivery in this version.
